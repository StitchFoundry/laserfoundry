## LaserFoundry 0.5.0

This release adds **a floating control box and a ring jog dial**, plus a safer connect and setup flow, start and finish positions, and more room for layers. Notes for 0.4.0 are folded in because 0.4.0 was never published.

> **This is still an early release.** Everything that talks to a machine has been tested only against the built-in simulator, not on real hardware. Read
> [`docs/hardware-bringup.md`](https://github.com/StitchFoundry/laserfoundry/blob/main/docs/hardware-bringup.md) before your first job: it covers safety, a laser-off motion test and
> first marks on scrap material. LaserFoundry is independent of, and unaffiliated with, other laser software vendors.

### What's new

- **Floating control box.** Start, Pause/Resume, Stop, Frame, Home, Unlock and Set/Go to origin in one box that floats over the canvas. Pin it to keep it in place and on top; its position is remembered per machine. Start shows why it is disabled when a job cannot start.
- **Ring jog dial.** A circle with four rings for speed: click or hold around the ring to jog that way, and the ring you are on sets the step and speed (the old arrow pad is still there as "Classic pad").
- **Finish position.** Choose where the head goes when a job ends: back to the start (the default), stay where it stopped, or a chosen point. The move always comes after the laser is switched off.
- **Marking on metal starters.** Two diode "mark only" starter presets for spray or paste on stainless or coated metal. Use the laser-off test grid on scrap first; values are starting points to verify.
- **30 layers** instead of 16. Old files keep their layers, and DXF import tells you when extra layers were merged.
- **Pick your laser from published spec sheets.** The setup wizard fills in bed size and limits from a list of machines, each linked to its source. Check an entry against its link before trusting it.
- **First-mark test grid.** A laser-off test grid with a safety checklist to check position and power on scrap before a real job.
- **Reconnecting works.** The serial port is closed on disconnect or a failed connect, a port you already granted reopens without the picker, and the web app shows an Unlock banner after an alarm. Status reports with the position fields in either order are read correctly.
- **Safer resume.** Resuming a job stopped with M5 no longer switches the laser back on, and a restarted job that stops again keeps its place.
- **Web app text and sign-in.** SVG text uses the loaded library fonts and reports text it cannot draw; the Foundry Account is the only sign-in.
- **Behind the scenes (not a user feature yet).** Sub-layers in the engine (several operations on one color layer; screens come next), camera groundwork, a staging switch and dependency updates.

### Known issues

- Tested on the built-in simulator, and connecting and jogging with the laser unplugged on one real controller. Not tested burning on real hardware.
- Sub-layers have no screens yet. Camera support is groundwork only.
