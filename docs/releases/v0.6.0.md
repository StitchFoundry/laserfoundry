## LaserFoundry 0.6.0

This release adds **a box maker and a fit test card**, **sub-layers you can see and edit**, a **hold-to-jog option**, and a safety change: **out-of-range laser power is refused instead of silently capped**.

> **This is still an early release.** Everything that talks to a machine has been tested only against the built-in simulator, not on real hardware. Read
> [`docs/hardware-bringup.md`](https://github.com/StitchFoundry/laserfoundry/blob/main/docs/hardware-bringup.md) before your first job: it covers safety, a laser-off motion test and
> first marks on scrap material. LaserFoundry is independent of, and unaffiliated with, other laser software vendors.

### What's new

- **Box maker.** A "Box maker" section in the Laser panel makes the panels of a finger-jointed box or open tray from its inside size, material thickness, kerf and finger width, laid out in rows ready to cut. One undo step. Test the joints on the fit test card (below) and a scrap cut before cutting good material.
- **Fit test card.** Tools > Fit test card makes a row of blocks with slots stepped around your material thickness, with the width engraved on each, so you can find the slot your material just fits and read off your kerf.
- **Sub-layers.** A layer can run several operations in order. The layer panel shows "Multi" for a layer with sub-layers, with per-sub-layer output, order, mode, speed, power, interval and passes. (They were engine-only in 0.5.0.)
- **Hold to jog.** A "Hold to jog" checkbox in the jog pad: press and hold to keep moving, release to stop (with a GRBL jog cancel). Not yet tested on real hardware.
- **Out-of-range power is refused.** A job or frame move with laser power outside 0 to 100 percent now stops with a clear error. Before, it was quietly capped at maximum power. If an old project or preset has power above 100, you will see the error and need to fix the value.
- **Nesting shows how much sheet you use.** After nesting, the status line says how much of the sheet the parts cover, for example "28% of the sheet used".
- **Font picker.** The text inspector's font list shows each font's name drawn in that font.
- **"Share it on MakerFoundry?" prompt.** After a project's first successful save or export, a small non-blocking window offers Share free, Sell it or Not now. It appears at most once per project, only where the app can reach the network, and "Don't ask again" or the "Offer to share after export" switch in the Publish to MakerFoundry window turns it off. Share and Sell only open MakerFoundry in your browser.
- **A new laser preset.** Ortur Laser Master 2 Pro S2 (10W module), with the bed size and power as Ortur states them. Thanks to Timo for the contribution. Check an entry against its link before trusting it.
- **Fixes.** Resume accepts G-code with parenthesised comments; older projects open with all 30 layers; the control box position is remembered on the desktop app; a broken raster image no longer crashes the test-grid sampler; a CLI test now looks in the right data folder on macOS.
- **Behind the scenes (not a user feature yet).** A bounded GeoJSON importer for the layered-maps work (no screens or map download yet), and dependency updates.

### Known issues

- Tested on the built-in simulator, and connecting and jogging with the laser unplugged on one real controller. Not tested burning on real hardware, including the box maker and fit test card.
- Hold to jog is untested on a real controller.
- Maps have no screens yet. Sub-layers part 3, nesting sheet-size presets and the font preview of your typed text are still to come.
